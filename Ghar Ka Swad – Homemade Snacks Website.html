Our Story

From our kitchen to yours.

Ghar Ka Swad began with a simple idea: the snacks we grew up eating were better than anything in a packet. What started as festive batches for family and friends is now a small home kitchen serving customers who miss the taste of real, homemade food.

We cook in small batches, use traditional recipes, and never use preservatives, so what reaches you is as honest as what we would serve our own family.
